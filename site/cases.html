Use cases.

Five live walkthroughs of Airy and Device Link.

Inline task Fix & complete Phone approval Pair & sync Session handoff

Ask at the prompt

Type what you want at the prompt and click the Airy icon. Airy reads the logs, finds the stopped container and fills in each command; press Enter, or let the rest run automatically.

Fix it, finish it

A misspelled unit fails and gets a fix icon. One click fills in the corrected command; pause mid-command and ghost text completes the line.

Approve from your phone

Away from your desk, your phone mirrors the desktop terminal. When Airy asks to restart a container, one tap approves it.

Scan once, bring your setup

Scan the desktop code to pair, then sync hosts, snippets and trusted keys. Saved passwords ask for confirmation on the desktop first.

Desktop to phone, and back

Tap a pending desktop tab or long-press a host to open it on the desktop. Commands typed on the phone run on both screens.

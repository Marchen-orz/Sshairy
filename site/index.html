Meet Airy.

An AI assistant beside your terminal and inside it, with 42 tools for hosts, files, commands, sessions, layouts and settings. Bring your own Anthropic or OpenAI-compatible model.

  • AgentDescribe a task and Airy works through it step by step.
  • Inline tasksAsk at the prompt. Airy explains each step in ✦ lines and fills in the next command for you to run.
  • Completion and fixesAI ghost text after the cursor, and a fix icon beside a failed command.
  • MCPClaude Code, Codex, Zcode, DeepSeek Harness, Antigravity and other MCP clients use the same 42 tools once you allow them.

Four permission modes: Observe (read-only with approval), Confirm (default, approve every read and command), Auto confirm (reviewer model), and Full auto.

Your servers, in your pocket.

Mirror desktop sessions, connect through the desktop, swap files and approve Airy from your phone. Pairing takes one scan, and the link is end-to-end encrypted.
